## 1. Platform role

NexHelp is intended to operate as a digital marketplace that helps customers submit home-service requests and connects them with eligible independent service providers. Unless the final contract expressly says otherwise, the service work is performed by the provider, not by NexHelp. The actual legal classification must reflect the real operating relationship and must not rely only on a contract label.

## 2. Account eligibility

[SET MINIMUM AGE AND IDENTITY POLICY.] The proposed pilot is for adult account holders. Customers must provide accurate contact and service details, keep account credentials secure and not impersonate another person.

## 3. Requests and booking confirmation

Submitting a request is a request for service, not a guarantee of provider availability or a confirmed appointment. NexHelp operations reviews the request and may contact the customer for clarification. A booking becomes confirmed only when the platform communicates a clear confirmation after an eligible provider has accepted the assignment. The customer must not treat an AI assessment or an unconfirmed request as a booking.

## 4. Service area and categories

The initial configured service zone is pincode 560102, with an HSR Layout focus. Requests outside the configured service area may be declined. Initial categories are plumbing and electrical work. Expansion requires a configured service area and an eligible provider roster.

## 5. Provider identity and work

Providers are subject to the verification steps disclosed by NexHelp. Verification status is not a guarantee of workmanship, safety, licensing or future conduct. Customers should verify the scope and price directly with the provider before work begins. Electrical work must be performed only by appropriately qualified/authorized personnel and in accordance with applicable safety requirements.

## 6. Price and payment

The platform currently records quotes and final amounts for operational purposes but does not process customer payments, hold funds or make provider payouts. Any quote must be communicated before work starts, and additional work or price changes require customer approval. These terms must be updated before any payment collection, commission, cancellation fee, deposit, wallet or refund feature is activated.

## 7. Cancellation, refunds and disputes

[INSERT COUNSEL-APPROVED CUSTOMER CANCELLATION, PROVIDER CANCELLATION, NO-SHOW, REFUND AND DISPUTE POLICY.] The current pilot has no integrated payment processor. Customers may raise an issue through [SUPPORT CHANNEL]. Complaints should receive a trackable reference and be handled within the applicable statutory timelines.

## 8. Optional AI triage

AI triage is optional and requires a separate affirmative consent action. It sends the issue description and selected category to Google Gemini. It is advisory only and may be inaccurate. It does not set prices, confirm appointments, dispatch providers or authorize repairs. Do not include personal, financial, health or identity details in the description. Booking remains available without AI.

## 9. Emergencies and safety

NexHelp is not an emergency-response service. For fire, smoke, gas leak, electric shock, exposed live wiring, major flooding or immediate risk to life, move away from the hazard and contact the relevant emergency service or utility. Do not attempt unsafe repairs based on app content or AI output.

## 10. Prohibited conduct

Users must not submit unlawful, fraudulent, abusive or misleading content; misuse provider/customer contact information; bypass platform safety controls; or request unsafe, illegal or unqualified work.
