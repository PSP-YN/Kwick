## 1. Scope and status

The provider may receive service requests through Kwick and, after accepting an offer, perform the agreed work for the customer. The legal relationship and classification must be determined from actual working practices, not merely this agreement. Nothing here authorizes work requiring a licence, permit or qualification the provider does not hold.

## 2. Eligibility and verification

Before activation, Kwick may verify identity, contact details, experience, trade competence, service area and any applicable licence/credential. The provider must supply accurate documents and notify Kwick of expiry, suspension, change or loss of qualification. Kwick may suspend dispatch while verification is incomplete or under review.

Do not request or retain full Aadhaar numbers, Aadhaar biometrics or Aadhaar e-KYC payloads as a routine verification step. Offer an alternative permitted identity document. Any UIDAI-based offline-verification process requires separate legal review, appropriate consent and compliance with UIDAI rules.

## 3. Safety and workmanship

The provider must:
- perform only work within their verified competence and legal authorization;
- comply with applicable electrical, plumbing, building, occupational-safety and consumer laws;
- use appropriate tools and protective equipment;
- stop work and escalate if a hazard exceeds their competence;
- never ask a customer to approach live wiring, fire, gas leaks or other dangerous conditions;
- explain the scope, parts, quote and any additional charges before work;
- obtain customer approval before additional work or price changes;
- protect customer property and personal information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, fraud, theft, intoxication or unsafe conduct.

## 4. Offers and service workflow

A request is not assigned until the provider accepts the offer through the authorized workflow. The provider must keep availability accurate, accept only work they can perform, update travel/start/completion status truthfully and promptly report cancellations or incidents. No automatic 15-minute offer expiry is currently implemented; do not rely on an automated timeout.

## 5. Pricing, invoices and tax

The provider must communicate a clear quote before work starts, issue invoices/receipts where required, state applicable taxes and provide accurate tax information. Commission, settlement, withholding, GST TCS and payment terms must be inserted only after tax counsel approves the operating model. Kwick currently does not collect or settle payments.

## 6. Customer data and confidentiality

Use customer name, phone, address, photos and service details only to fulfil the accepted job and resolve related support issues. Do not export, sell, copy, retain or reuse customer information for unrelated marketing or off-platform solicitation. Protect devices and accounts, promptly report suspected loss or disclosure, and delete local copies when no longer needed.
